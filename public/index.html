How to Play

🏠
Create or Join a Room

One player creates a room and shares the 6-letter code. The other joins using the code.

📝
Set Up Your Grid

Each player fills a 5×5 grid with numbers 1–25. Place them manually or use Random.

🪙
Coin Toss

The server flips a coin to decide who goes first.

🔢
Call Numbers

On your turn, call any uncalled number (1–25). It gets marked on both grids.

🏆
Win!

Complete 5 lines (rows, columns, or diagonals) to spell BINGO and win!
